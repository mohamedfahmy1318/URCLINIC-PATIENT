# UrClinic Data Deletion Request

Effective date: 2026-04-04

UrClinic users may request deletion of their account and personal data.

This page applies to:

- UrClinic Patient (com.urclinic.patient)
- UrClinic Employee (com.urclinic.employee)

## 1) How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion by:

- Using the in-app account deletion feature (if available)
- Sending an email from your registered account email to: findogroup5@gmail.com
- Submitting request via support channel: https://urclinic.findosystem.com/contact-us

## 2) Required Information

Please include:

- Full name
- Registered email
- Registered phone number
- App used (Patient or Employee)
- Reason (optional)

## 3) Verification

For security, we may verify account ownership before deletion.

## 4) Deletion Timeline

- Initial acknowledgment: within 3 business days
- Processing target: within 30 days, unless legal requirements require longer

## 5) What Will Be Deleted

- Account profile data
- App usage data linked to the account where deletion is permitted
- Push token and related app identity metadata

## 6) What May Be Retained

Certain data may be retained where required by law, compliance, fraud prevention, dispute handling, security, or medical/financial record obligations.

## 7) Contact

For deletion requests:

- Email: findogroup5@gmail.com
- Website: https://urclinic.findosystem.com
